# Daily quest variety

## Daily special expeditions

Normal dungeons rotate through twelve identities: Ragefire Chasm, The Deadmines, Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery, Uldaman, Zul’Farrak, Razorfen Downs, Maraudon, The Sunken Temple and Blackrock Depths.

Daily raids rotate through Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Zul’Gurub, Ruins of Ahn’Qiraj, Naxxramas and Karazhan. Daily bosses rotate through Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, Doomwalker, Sha of Anger, Oondasta and Nalak. Each has named encounters from the appropriate WoW instance or boss.

Selection is deterministic by game day. Reloading or refreshing cannot reroll it. Unaccepted special offers from earlier days are replaced on the next day's board, while ordinary contract expiry remains intact. Already dispatched reports retain their identities, rolls and rewards. Sunken Reliquary is no longer the daily placeholder.

Normal Dungeon/Raid/Boss rank, DC, rewards, reputation, XP, party requirements, duration, loot and encounter counts still come from the existing factories. The change replaces identity/description/encounter names, not those formulas. Normal dungeons and daily bosses remain one stage; normal raids retain their existing encounter structure. The daily dungeon dispatch limit still prevents replacement copies that day.

A daily dungeon does not discover its named world location and does not grant its exclusive world-dungeon materials. Permanent discovered dungeons, world bosses and world raids retain fixed identities, ranks, stages, gates and completion rules. In particular, all thirty fixed-rank dungeons preserve their signature materials.

New reports snapshot daily boss names and individual encounter tiers so the full report can display names and outcome-appropriate fight accounts without recalculating old results.

## Contracts and six-stat coverage

Added 48 ordinary titles (eight for each base category), plus 42 titles across the seven discovery-unlocked categories: bounty hunts, arena challenges, smuggler interdiction, diplomatic courier, arcane survey, caravan defence and Scourge cleansing. These categories retain their existing discovery/reputation unlocks. Discovered contract sites remain in the pool.

Saved shuffled title bags reduce repetition within each eligible category. Existing board titles are avoided where the pool permits; repeats remain possible once a pool is exhausted or all its titles are already offered. Refreshing the board does not consume or reroll existing offers.

Every freshly generated daily board has at least one STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA contract. Existing generated ranks are retained. Where necessary, redundant unaccepted offers are assigned an appropriate existing base archetype and its existing numerical formulas:

- STR: maintenance/practical assistance.
- DEX: retrieval/salvage.
- CON: escort/delivery.
- INT: investigation/observation.
- WIS: rescue/search contracts.
- CHA: escort/delivery with social checks.

The existing reward boost applies once, and hero compensation, XP and expiry scaling remain. Coverage does not continuously replenish consumed offers: it runs once for a day's generated board. A mid-day save adopting the patch can receive a one-time top-up to six unaccepted contracts if needed. Pending or returned reports are not modified. Permanent exploration and gathering keep their purpose and current check mechanics.

## Validation

Browser checks cover twelve consecutive days with all six stats, 12/6/6 distinct special rotations, Hall levels 1–7 across four reputation bands, existing rank/DC/reward/party formulas, the reward boost applied once, contract title diversity, unique quest IDs, stable repeated refreshes, no same-day contract refill, no automatic discovery, all thirty fixed world dungeons, named full reports and save/reload of offers and sealed results. Existing daily-dungeon, staged-world, report-presentation and offline-update regressions also pass.
