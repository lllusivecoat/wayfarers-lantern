Fury uses Titan Grip: each signature greatsword is held in one hand and contributes the normal one-handed bonus, keeping its pair equal to a same-strength two-handed weapon. Total gear readiness remains capped at +6.

Unpaired weapon families: axes, crossbows, pistols and wands. Healer tomes are also unpaired offhands. Legacy recipes and inventory records are retained for compatibility; the weapon crafting selector offers only the 40 assigned patterns.

Compatible equipped legacy weapons adopt their hero’s signature pattern, preserving IDs, tiers, bonus amounts, quality, durability and ownership. Original names and stats are retained as metadata. Incompatible equipment returns to inventory. Respecialising requires the new spec’s weapon. Shield and focus bonuses use their wearer’s primary attribute. Already dispatched reports keep their sealed results.
