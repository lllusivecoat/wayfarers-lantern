Recruit · equip · promote

Heroes of the Lantern

Ten ranks · level + successful next-rank quest
Rank ladder

F → E → D → C → B → A → S → SS → Legendary → Renown

Heroes earn XP based on quest type and rank. Exceptional success gives 150%, qualifying partial success 50%, and failure no base XP. Overlevelled heroes earn less from easy quests. Levels 1–4 need 350 XP each, levels 5–9 need 400; from level 10 the cost starts at 450 and rises by 50 per level. Excess XP carries forward. A hero who meets the level threshold is promoted after personally joining and succeeding on a quest at the next rank; permanent quests (gathering expeditions and the World Dungeon) never count. Recruitment fees by rank: F free, E 20, D 35, C 75, B 150, A 500 gp. Outreach candidates always arrive at rank F.

Guild reputation unlocks higher-tier contracts. A hero may join a quest at their rank or one rank above it.

Recruit, assign, and train

Each hero rolls one d20 + readiness. Success requires at least two-thirds of the party to meet the DC; one-third earns a partial success. A natural 20 improves the result, while a natural 1 worsens it. Full dungeon and raid parties are strongly recommended.
Repeatable expeditions

Permanent Quests

Always available · gathering and standing work
The guild pays an organising fee upfront for each expedition; there is no client payment. Fees scale with rank and party size and are not refunded on failure. Gathering missions can be sent again whenever members are ready.
